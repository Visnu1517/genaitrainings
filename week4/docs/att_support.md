# AT&T — Business Services, Support, and Policies

## Business services

AT&T offers business solutions including mobile plans for teams, internet, cloud
connectivity, and collaboration tools for small and enterprise customers.
Business accounts can add or remove lines as staffing changes.
Dedicated internet options are available for businesses that need guaranteed bandwidth.

## Customer support

Customers can get help through the AT&T website, the myAT&T app, phone support, and AT&T
retail stores.
Account holders can manage billing, plan changes, device upgrades, and troubleshooting
through online self-service tools.
The myAT&T app can be used to view current usage, pay a bill, and check the status of an
order.
Appointments at AT&T retail stores can be scheduled in advance through att.com.

## Billing and account management

Bills are issued monthly and can be paid online, through the myAT&T app, by phone, or in a
retail store.
AutoPay can be enabled so the balance is charged automatically each billing cycle.
Paperless billing is available for customers who prefer electronic statements.

## General policies

Service terms, pricing, promotions, and device offers can change and may vary by region.
For account-specific details such as an exact bill amount, contract end date, or outage
status, customers should be directed to official AT&T channels rather than given a guess.
Coverage depends on location, device, and network conditions.

If a user asks about AT&T and the answer is not covered in the knowledge base, respond that
the information is not available and suggest contacting AT&T support or visiting att.com.
