# AT&T — Company Overview and Consumer Services

## Company overview

AT&T Inc. is a major American telecommunications company headquartered in Dallas, Texas.
AT&T provides wireless, broadband, and business communication services across the United
States.

## Consumer wireless

AT&T offers postpaid and prepaid mobile phone plans for individuals and families.
Plans may include unlimited talk, text, and data options, with features such as 5G access
on compatible devices.
Customers can bring their own device or purchase phones through AT&T with installment or
full-payment options.
Family plans allow multiple lines on a single account, and per-line pricing typically
decreases as more lines are added.
Prepaid plans do not require an annual contract or a credit check.

## Home internet

AT&T provides home internet services, including fiber (AT&T Fiber) and other broadband
options depending on location.
Fiber plans advertise high-speed internet suitable for streaming, gaming, and working from
home.
Availability varies by address; not all services are offered in every area.
Customers can check whether service is available at a specific address using the address
lookup tool on att.com.
Professional installation may be required for new fiber service.
