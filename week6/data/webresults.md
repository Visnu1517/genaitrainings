## Industry Context

- Total US wireless market is effectively a three-player market.
- Growth is driven by switching, not new subscribers; the market is saturated.
- Convergence (bundling home internet with wireless) is the main battleground.
- Enterprise and private 5G are the fastest-growing revenue segments.
