#### The Opportunity
**AT&T can convert fiber reach into ARPU and market cap premium through aggressive bundling.**

**Recommendation 1a: Accelerate Fiber Attach Among Wireless Base**

- **Target:** Increase fiber attach from 19% to 35% within 24 months (9.6M net new customers)

**Recommendation 1b: Launch "Home + Mobile" Family Plans**

- Package fiber (broadband + video) + multiple wireless lines with progressive discounts
  - Example: $150/mo for fiber + 3 lines (vs. $280+ separately)
- **Why it works:** Verizon myPlan is **granular but fragmented**; T-Mobile has no home broadband offer. AT&T's fiber footprint allows **integrated billing and retention.**
- **Expected outcome:** Reduce churn from 0.85% to ~0.75% (T-Mobile's 0.91% is higher because they lack bundling stickiness).
